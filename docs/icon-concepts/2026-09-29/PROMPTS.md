```text
Primary request: Create ONE polished icon exploring a containment-seal launch emblem. Actual transparent background outside the solid dark circular seal. Ring and arrows must be visually simple and bold enough to survive at 32 pixels. Strong readable silhouette at small size.
Color palette: charcoal #151A1D, warm ivory #F2EEE4, hazard orange #F28C28.
Constraints: icon only, one symbol, no words or letters, no numbers, no watermark, no title, no presentation board, no scene, no extra surrounding icons. No microscopic lines, grunge, particles, dramatic glow, perspective tilt, chrome or photographic texture. Preserve true transparency outside the emblem.
```
